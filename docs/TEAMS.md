# Teams and the Hypno-shroom

The original marks "mind-controlled" with a single boolean on the zombie, `mMindControlled`, checked separately in over twenty functions. The remake uses teams instead: plants and zombies each belong to a team, different teams are hostile to each other, and the Hypno-shroom moves the zombie that bites it to the plants' team. Every check goes through "is it hostile", with no branches for mind control any more. The original's behaviour comes from PvZ-Portable (the 1073 source); in this area 1096 only adds the Disco is Undead achievement (see [Achievements](ACHIEVEMENTS.md)).

## The original's behaviour

### The Hypno-shroom bitten (`Zombie::AnimateChewSound`)

When a zombie bites (on the sound frame of its chewing animation) and what it bites is an awake Hypno-shroom (`!mIsAsleep`; asleep in the day it does nothing):

1. It plays `FOLEY_FLOOP`, and the Hypno-shroom dies (`Plant::Die`).
2. `StartMindControlled`: it plays `SOUND_MINDCONTROLLED` and sets `mMindControlled = true`; a Dancing Zombie empties its backup dancers and a backup dancer leaves its Dancing Zombie, while other zombies let go of their related zombie (the bobsled team and so on).
3. It puts `PARTICLE_MIND_CONTROL` one layer above the zombie (`mRenderOrder + 1`), at `(mPosX + 60, mPosY + 40)`.
4. `TrySpawnLevelAward`: if it is the last hostile zombie on the lawn, the award drops from it.
5. It sets the speed `mVelX = 0.17` and the animation `mAnimTicksPerFrame = 18`. But the zombie is eating at that moment, and the next time it finds nothing to eat, `StopEating` → `StartWalkAnim` → `PickRandomSpeed` picks a random speed for its zombie type again (as every zombie does when it stops eating and walks on), so the 0.17 has no effect in practice, and the mind-controlled zombie walks right at the ordinary speed of its kind.

A mind-controlled zombie biting a zombie uses `FOLEY_CHOMP_SOFT`.

### Once mind-controlled

| The original's function | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `IsWalkingBackwards` | Walks right (`mPosX += speed`), drawn mirrored |
| `GetZombieAttackRect` | The attack range is mirrored to the right: `x = mWidth − x − width` |
| `UpdateEating` | Each bite first looks for a hostile zombie (`FindZombieTarget`) and bites it if there is one; a mind-controlled zombie does not bite plants |
| `FindZombieTarget` | In the same row, on a different team, not dead, not in the air or underground, overlapping the attack range by ≥ 20 (≥ 0 when the other is eating) |
| `EatZombie` | The other takes `TakeDamage(DAMAGE_PER_EAT, 9)` and it starts eating; when the other's body health reaches zero it plays `SOUND_GULP` |
| `EffectedByDamage` | Plant damage (projectiles, blasts, bites) does not hit mind-controlled zombies; only attacks with `DAMAGES_ONLY_MINDCONTROLLED` do (none in the base game yet) |
| `CanBeChilled` | Cannot be chilled (this project makes the chill follow hostile attacks instead; see "Chill" below) |
| `CheckForBoardEdge` | Disappears silently once it walks right past `x = 850` (`DieNoLoot`) |
| `DrawReanim`, `DrawZombiePart` | The color `(128, 64, 192)` multiplied, then the same color added on top (`ZOMBIE_MINDCONTROLLED_COLOR`) |
| `OverrideParticleColor` | The head, arm and other particles that fall off are tinted and overlaid the same way |
| `AreEnemyZombiesOnScreen`, `CountZombiesOnScreen`, `TotalZombiesHealthInWave` | Not counted: no effect on the level-complete check or on the waves' health threshold |
| Lawn mower | Does not crush mind-controlled zombies (`EffectedByDamage(127)`) |

The other branches, which concern only special zombies or plants (the Gargantuar, the Yeti, the ladder, the Squash and Jalapeno zombies, the Magnet-shroom and so on), will be wired to teams once the content they concern is implemented.

## Design

### Teams

- Two fixed teams: `plants` and `zombies`. The player is `plants`.
- Plant and zombie components carry `team`, by default `plants` and `zombies` respectively; it is saved and sent to the client in snapshots.
- Projectiles and blasts carry the team of whoever launched them.
- There is only one hostility rule: different teams are hostile (`isHostile`), and every check calls it.
- The team decides facing: the `zombies` team faces left, the `plants` team right. Zombies walk the way they face: a `zombies` zombie that gets into the house loses the level, and a `plants` one past `x = 850` disappears silently. Plants' attack ranges, blast centers, projectile directions and bowling directions are mirrored by facing (across `Plant::mWidth = 80`).

### Changed checks

- Plants' target searches (shooters, the Fume-shroom's area attack, the Chomper, the Potato Mine, bowling), projectile hits and blasts: they act on zombies and plants of a hostile team and never touch their own. With the default teams, plants only ever meet hostile zombies, as in the original (the original's plant attacks pass over mind-controlled zombies, `EffectedByDamage`).
- Hitting hostile plants is an extension (the original has no plants on the zombies' side), with rules following the original's zombie peas (`Projectile::DoImpact`): it takes the plant's health straight off and flashes it white for 25 ticks, whatever state the plant is in; at zero health the plant is handled as eaten (an Explode-o-nut still explodes). A projectile hitting a plant needs an overlap above 8 (`FindCollisionTargetPlant`), the plant's body being `Plant::GetPlantRect`; projectiles fly over the low plants marked `low: true` in their plant definitions (the Puff-shroom, Sun-shroom, Potato Mine and others the original's zombie peas skip), and shooters that fire projectiles do not target them either, while area attacks still hit them. The Potato Mine's checks against hostile plants reach half a cell further forward (`targeting.plantReach: 40`): its trigger range and its blast's check against plants both move 40 forward, so a hostile plant in the next cell sets it off and is blown up; its checks against zombies and the position of the blast effect are unchanged. The Chomper swallows a hostile plant whole (its state records `ability.prey: 'plant'`, and while it chews and swallows, the client hides the two tracks of the zombie's arm in its animation, the presentation definition's `parts.prey`); a plant zombies cannot bite (a lit Cherry Bomb, a Potato Mine rising or risen) the Chomper cannot bite either, and it misses. A risen Potato Mine sets itself off when a hostile plant is within half a cell in front of it; blasts take health by their damage, and `kills` counts only zombies; a bowled nut hitting a plant does not count toward `hits`; when two hostile rolling nuts collide, both are resolved at once, each takes the other's impact damage and bounces off, an Explode-o-nut goes off, and the result does not depend on which is resolved first. `/plantinvincible` blocks this damage too.
- Zombie bites: a zombie first looks for a hostile zombie in front (by the same rules as `FindZombieTarget`), then for a hostile plant; zombies do not bite their own team's plants.
- Zombie movement and attack ranges are mirrored by their team's direction.
- Waves, level completion and dropping the level award: only zombies of the `zombies` team are counted.
- Chill: it acts only on a hostile team and follows the attack (a Snow Pea's pea only hits hostile targets). The original's `CanBeChilled` hard-codes that mind-controlled zombies cannot be chilled only because only plants chill in the original; that rule is dropped here, so an enemy Snow Pea's peas can slow zombies of the `plants` team, and zombies and plants that are both mind-controlled and chilled or frozen (which never happens in the original, where mind-controlled zombies cannot be frozen) take the average of the two colors, (102, 70, 224), so that both states show (`client/view/tints.ts`'s `entityTint`), and so do the parts they drop. The Ice-shroom freezes only a hostile team (see the next item). A hostile Snow Pea's pea that hits a plant chills it too (an extension; the original's plants are never chilled): for as long as it would chill a zombie, keeping the longer of the two, and meanwhile the plant skips one tick in two, so shooting, charging, producing sun, ability timers (fuses, the Potato Mine arming, the Chomper digesting and so on), the blink countdown and bowling all run at half speed; its animation, like a chilled zombie's body, is advanced by the server every tick at half rate (`plantPace`, `CHILLED_ANIMATION_FACTOR`), the snapshot carries the halved rate, and the client plays it as sent; it plays the chill sound as it is first chilled (`FOLEY_FROZEN`, as `Zombie::ApplyChill` does); the client turns it blue down the same path as a chilled zombie (`ReanimPlayer.setTint`, `plants/chill.ts`, the `plantChilled` event, the save field `chilledTicks`). `/plantinvincible` blocks only damage, not the chill.
- Whether something can be chilled depends on its type (`server/combat/cold.ts`, one set of rules for plants and zombies): `unchillable` in its definition means it is not slowed, and so not frozen either, and a Snow Pea's slowing does nothing to it (the Torchwood; the original's Zomboni, the bobsled team pulling its sled, and so on); `unfreezable` means it is not frozen, only slowed, and does not take the freeze's 20 damage either (the original's `HitIceTrap` slows first and returns when `CanBeFrozen` is false). A plant's definition writes either only as `true`, at all times; where a plant is kept from the cold or the ice only at times, its kind decides it (`Plant.keptFromCold`, `Plant.keptFromIce`: a Potato Mine rising). A zombie's writes `true` or a list of zombie states (`[states…]`), kept so only in those states. In the base game the `unfreezable` ones are the Cherry Bomb, the Ice-shroom, both bowling nuts, the Grave Buster, a Potato Mine rising (`rising`) and a pole vaulter in the air (`airborne`); the Doom-shroom is too; the Squash, Blover and Jalapeno, once they have locked on, get the same when they are implemented, and the Torchwood gets `unchillable`. Zombies that are dying, charred, squashed or rising from a grave, and plants already destroyed, are never slowed or frozen, whatever their type.
- The Ice-shroom: it freezes every zombie and every plant hostile to it (`combat/freeze.ts`'s `freezeHostiles`). The original's freeze spares mind-controlled zombies (`CanBeChilled`), which are exactly the only zombies on its side in the original; what it spares here is its own side. A `zombies`-team Ice-shroom freezes the zombies of the `plants` team, and the `plants` team's plants too (an extension; the original freezes no plants): plants, like zombies, are slowed for 2000 ticks, frozen for 400–600 ticks (300–400 if already slowed or frozen) and take 20 damage (`damagePlant`, with a flash); while frozen they skip every tick (`plantSkipsTick`), so shooting, producing sun, ability timers and blinking all pause, the animation stands still (`plantPace` is 0), the particles on the plant stay where they are too (the Grave Buster's chewing, `ParticlePlayer.setPaused`), and a Cherry Bomb stops shaking; it can still be eaten and hit. The ice is drawn by the plant's default position, in the middle of the cell, its top edge level with the top edge of the plant's shadow (the presentation's `iceTrap.plant`, (13, 51), just as a zombie's ice lines its top edge up with the zombie's shadow at 92; plants stand higher than the zombies of the same row, their shadow at 51), the ice shard particles at (40, 65), mirrored left and right along with the plant. If the plant is destroyed while frozen (`destroyEatenPlant`), the ice shatters; if a lawn mower crushes it, the ice is simply removed. The board records 300 ticks for the frozen side (`LevelState.iceTrap`). Save fields: `frozenTicks` (plants and zombies), `level.iceTrap`.
- Lawn mowers: they do not crush zombies of the `plants` team (the original's `EffectedByDamage(127)`, the same as plant attacks).
- The shovel: it can dig up only plants of the `plants` team; plants of the `zombies` team are not highlighted and cannot be dug up (as the Grave Buster cannot be dug up).

### The Hypno-shroom

- A general plant ability, `mindControl: true`: when a hostile zombie bites it while it is awake (on the sound frame of the chewing animation, `Zombie::AnimateChewSound`), that bite does not count as a bite, the plant vanishes at once (its health cleared, so no other zombie bites it in the same tick), and the zombie that bit it changes to the plant's team. The data is written in the plant definition, not decided by the plant's ID.
- An extension (the original's plants do not eat plants): when a plant bites an awake hostile Hypno-shroom (the Chomper, at the place in `plants/abilities.ts` where it swallows plants), the plant that bit it likewise changes to the Hypno-shroom's team; that bite does not count as swallowing, it is drawn back as a miss (`missed`), goes back to `ready` afterwards, and neither chews nor digests (`chomperBit`'s `ate` is false); a Hypno-shroom asleep in the day is eaten as usual. Zombies and plants go through the same `mindControl` (`zombies/mindControl.ts`; whether it works is decided by `mindControls`).
- On changing team: the speed does not change, and is picked again at random when it stops eating and walks on, as for every zombie (`Zombie.pickSpeed`, i.e. `PickRandomSpeed`); the zombie or plant records `mindControlled` (saved, and sent to the client in snapshots; only mind-controlled ones are tinted, and ones planted or placed on the other side do not count); it emits the `mindControlled` event (`entityId`, `plantId`, `typeId`, `team`); if the mind-controlled zombie was the last hostile zombie, the level award drops from it (`tryDropLevelAward`).
- The Disco is Undead achievement observes `mindControlled` through the `pvz:mind-controlled` trigger type, set to the Dancing Zombie (`zombies: ["pvz:dancer"]`): a zombie of a listed type coming over to the `plants` side earns it, while a plant that swallowed a Hypno-shroom, or a zombie brought over to the `zombies` side, does not.

### Presentation

- Zombies of the `plants` team are mirrored as in the original's `Zombie::UpdateReanim`: the body moves right 90 and then flips, whatever the clip's offsets, so zombies that face right in the original (mind-controlled ones, and later the Digger, the Yeti and so on) all look as they do in the original. Being put down facing right, which the original does not have, is corrected separately so that it lines up with the other zombies: a zombie gameplay definition's `facingRightOffsetX` makes it stand further right when put down, and the held cursor and the cell preview move by the same; in a seed packet the zombie presentation definition's `packet.facingRightOffsetX` is used. Both of the pole vaulter's values come from the original's data: its `GetDrawPos` is −6 horizontally, and its seed packet x is 5 pixels left of the plain zombie's (−8 against −3, at scale 0.35, i.e. its body is 14.29 further right in its own coordinates), so put down it is 6 + 14.29 = 20.29, and in the seed packet 2 × 14.29 = 28.57. The Football zombie's seed packet scale (0.33) differs from the plain zombie's (0.35), so its value is worked out instead by lining the two bodies up on the seed packet's center line (x = 25 of its 50 width): its `GetDrawPos` only moves it up, its seed packet x is −7, and in its own coordinates its body is (25 + 7) / 0.33 − (25 + 3) / 0.35 = 16.97 further right than the plain zombie's, so it stands 16.97 further right when put down; at the same scale this works out the same as the pole vaulter's. The shadow uses the presentation definition's `shadow.backwardsX`. The head, arm, flag and armor particles that fall off, and the charred zombie after burning, also flip to face right; for mind-controlled zombies the original mirrors only the position and does not flip the particles, which is fixed here as a bug. The crumbs from chewing a Wall-nut flip as well.
- The mind-controlled look comes with the Hypno-shroom: the color `(128, 64, 192)` multiplied and overlaid, the limbs and helmets that fall off tinted the same, and a soft chewing sound for biting zombies (the original decides all of these by `mMindControlled`). `plants`-team zombies put down from a seed packet or with `/zombie ... plants` are not mind-controlled; they look like ordinary zombies, only facing right.
- The team-change event plays `FOLEY_FLOOP`, `SOUND_MINDCONTROLLED` and `PARTICLE_MIND_CONTROL`; zombies biting zombies use the soft chewing sound, and the gulp sound plays when the other falls.

## Steps

1. Done: team data, `isHostile`, saves and snapshots (`shared/content/teams.ts`, shared by the client and the server).
2. Done: the existing checks (plant targets, projectiles, blasts, chill, waves, level completion, getting into the house) changed to go by team.
3. Done: zombies biting zombies (through shields, with no white flash; the events `zombieChewedZombie` and `zombieAteZombie`), and moving and leaving by their team's direction.
4. Done: plants attacking hostile plants (the event `plantHit`; `projectileImpact` and `bowlingImpact` carry `target: 'plant'` when they hit a plant, and `chomperBit` carries `plantId` when it swallows a plant).
5. Done: the Hypno-shroom ability and the team-change event (`server/zombies/mindControl.ts`).
6. Done: zombie mirroring, mirroring of `zombies`-team plants and their projectiles, plants' hit flash, the bite and gulp sounds; mind-controlled zombies and the heads, arms and flags they drop are tinted the mind-control color (`ReanimPlayer.setTint`; one chilled or frozen as well takes the average of the two colors, as under the cold above), mind-controlled zombies biting zombies use the soft chewing sound, and a team change plays `FOLEY_FLOOP`, `SOUND_MINDCONTROLLED` and `PARTICLE_MIND_CONTROL` (`StageEventRegistry`'s `mindControlled`: a zombie's at (60, 40), a plant's at its center (40, 40)). Mind-controlled plants are tinted the mind-control color too (`client/view/tints.ts`'s `entityTint`, shared by plants and zombies). Plants planted on the `zombies` team from the start are not tinted; a `zombies`-team projectile's shadow is mirrored with it (`ProjectileView`).
7. Done: `tests/server/teams.test.ts` covers walking right, not being attacked by plants, zombies biting each other, vanishing past the right edge, restoring from a save, the two sides' plants shooting each other, blasts and the Chomper hitting only hostile plants, zombies biting only hostile plants, mind control (the team change, speed, saving, and `plants`-team zombies that were put down not counting as mind-controlled), no effect in the day, and the award dropping when the last zombie is mind-controlled.

Seed packets carry a team too, meaning "which side what it puts down belongs to": plant seeds default to `plants`, zombie seeds to `zombies` (`seedTeam`), and only a team that differs from the default is written into `seedBank.packets[].team` (saved, and in the snapshot's `seedPackets[].team` and the held `cursor.team`). A `zombies` packet of a plant seed plants a plant of the zombies' team, and a `plants` packet of a zombie seed puts down a zombie of the plants' team; the client draws what is in the packet, in hand and in the placement preview the other way round (`PlantPreview` / `ZombiePreview`'s `setMirrored`). For now one is swapped in with `/seed {slot} {seed_name} {team}`.

Demo level: `/level pvz:teams-demo`. A level's `initialPlants` can give `"team": "zombies"` to preset plants of the `zombies` team.

The backup dancers a Dancing Zombie calls up take on its team and its mind-control flag (the original copies only the mind-control flag), and their places flip left and right with its facing; a mind-controlled Dancing Zombie lets its old backup dancers go and from then on calls up its own side's, and a mind-controlled backup dancer leaves its old Dancing Zombie. A crater's team only decides which way it is drawn: no side's plants can be planted on a crater; the crater card belongs to the plants by default, and a crater a Doom-shroom leaves belongs to that Doom-shroom's side.

Debugging: `/zombie <type> <row> <col> plants` spawns a zombie of the `plants` team, `/plant <type> <row> <col> zombies` spawns a plant of the `zombies` team, and `/seed <slot> <type> zombies` swaps in a seed packet of the `zombies` team.
