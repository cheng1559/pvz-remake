# Gameplay

What the game does, level by level and entity by entity, and where each behaviour comes from in the original. Function and constant names (`Board::PickZombieWaves`, `SEED_ZOMBIE_*`) are the original's; PvZ-Portable is the readable reference. How the code is put together is in [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md).

Adventure 1-1 through 2-8 is connected. The debug CLI is available in Debug and Release builds: press `/`, or double-tap the bottom-right corner in mobile mode. See [Debug CLI](DEBUG_CLI.md). The levels after 2-8 remain unimplemented. The main menu uses original image buttons and its Reanim entrance; Options has working settings and in-game actions. Almanac and Store have their own content and interactions; other game modes remain unimplemented. Refer to the workspace's `pvz-remake` when porting their behavior.

## Adventure

Adventure 1-5 keeps Crazy Dave's two dialogues and the digging up of three Peashooters in `ShovelTutorial`; the client's `CrazyDaveView` only plays his animation, shows the original's lines and sends the interaction commands. After the lesson come eight waves of Wall-nut Bowling, the conveyor supplying bowling and explosive nuts 85:15. The award is the Potato Mine.

Adventure 1-6 through 1-10 follows the reference `Challenge.h`, `Board::PickZombieWaves`, `Challenge::UpdateConveyorBelt`, and `LawnApp::GetAwardSeedForLevel`: 10/20/10/20/20 waves with a flag every ten waves, pole vaulters introduced in 1-6, and bucketheads (without pole vaulters) in 1-8. Levels 1-6 and 1-7 take every available seed (five and six, which fit the bank); 1-8 and 1-9 choose six from the shared catalog. The available seeds are the ones the player owns (`LawnApp::HasSeedType`): the profile keeps them in `seeds`, a won level adds its award seed and a store purchase of kind `seed` adds the plant it sells, and the gameplay settings' `seedBank.startingSeeds` (the Peashooter) are owned from the start. The level gets the list as `ownedSeeds` in the start packet and keeps it in its save; without a player every catalog seed is available. The chooser, and an open chooser in a save, accept only those, while a confirmed bank may hold any installed seed (`/seed`); an award's packet shows the seed's own cost.

Rewards are Snow Pea, Chomper, Repeater, the zombie note, and Puff-shroom. The note uses its own dropped image, in-place fade, 500-tick black transition, paper sound and `pageImages` layout. Level 1-10 uses the existing conveyor with seven free plant types, no sky sun, a first wave at tick 100 and first supply at tick 200. Duplicate/repeated supply weights and the last supplied seed survive saves. The Puff-shroom award now leads into 2-1. The shovel follows bank width (`Board::GetSeedBankExtraWidth`: x=456 for up to six slots, x=609 for ten) and stays hidden while seeds are chosen, showing once the intro is over. Preview zombie tooltips reuse the shared button/tooltip controls and localized presentation `nameKey`, confined to the preview area and hidden once the choice is confirmed, the game paused or a dialog open. Once the player has the almanac (`unlocks.almanac`), a preview zombie listed in it (matched by `nameKey`) adds "click to view" to its tooltip and, clicked, plays the tap and opens the zombie page on its entry over the paused level; Close returns to the chooser (SeedChooserScreen::MouseDown, AlmanacDialog::ShowZombie). The chooser also shows the original Almanac and Shop buttons at (560, 572) and (680, 572) once the almanac and store are unlocked; the store opened from it says "Go back".

Adventure 2-1 reuses the shared level and chooser flow: a night board, 50 starting sun, six chosen packets and ten waves. First adventure plays Dave lines 201–207 before choosing; no sky-sun timer is created. Puff-shroom stores its original random planting offsets (x=-5…4, y=-3…2) in the entity position and uses a centered half-size night shadow. It costs zero sun, refreshes in 750 ticks, attacks within 230px beyond its attack origin and fires spores that expire before collision at age 75. It sleeps by day. The newspaper zombie is fixed in waves 6 and 10. Its newspaper absorbs 150 damage, then it gasps at 8fps before walking at 0.89–0.91, roaring only with its head on and at most ten headed zombies alive; chilling also slows the gasp. These entities use the existing shooting, damage and save systems.

Sun-shroom, Fume-shroom and Grave Buster are installed (unlocked from 2-2, 2-3 and 2-4) and the almanac uses their level animations. The Sun-shroom (25 sun) drops 15-sun small sun at half scale, grows after 12000 awake ticks through the 100-tick `anim_grow`, during which it does not produce, then drops normal sun; asleep by day it neither grows nor produces. The Fume-shroom (75 sun) fires every 136–150 ticks after a 50-tick windup, dealing 20 to every zombie within 340px of its attack origin in the row; damage passes the newspaper and still hits the body in full (`hitsShieldAndBody`), and the fume cloud appears 35 ticks into the shot. The Grave Buster (75 sun) can only be planted on a grave, with the original advice, and only on one of the other side (`cell-has-own-grave` otherwise, without advice): the plants' on the zombies' graves, a zombies' side one on the plants' side's; zombies can eat it, but the shovel cannot dig it. It lands for 47–70 ticks (`anim_land` at the body's random 10–15 fps), then eats for 400 ticks while sinking 30px and eating the stone from the top, and finally removes itself and its grave, dropping a loot piece (`DropLootPiece` at factor 12) only when it ate a grave and is on the plants' side; the original drops one even with no grave, which only `/plant` could reach. Sun-shroom growth and Grave Buster progress are plant ability states and are saved. A sun producer on the zombies' side (Sunflower, Sun-shroom) makes no sun: each time it would, it takes what that sun is worth (25; a small Sun-shroom's 15) from the player's bank, down to 0 (the `sunDrained` event), glowing as it would and flashing the sun counter red without the buzzer.

Graves occupy one cell in column 7, one in column 8 and two in column 9, with distinct random rows. Ordinary planting is rejected with the original advice; grave cells suppress placement previews and mobile guides. They rise with dirt particles and sound once the intro pans back to the lawn, delaying the mowers and Ready-Set-Plant by 100 ticks. Their layout, appearance, rise delay and pending spawn timer survive saves. Two hundred ticks after the final wave, each grave spawns one zombie picked by the level's `graveZombies` weights, with a 150-tick emergence; none rises once the award has dropped. `GraveView` draws the original stone/mound atlas and `ZombieView` clips the emerging body, by the presentation's `graves.zombie` (how deep it starts and its clip, the original's `Zombie::UpdateZombieRiseFromGrave`, `GetDrawPos` and `DrawReanim` values), which a resource pack may change. Restore synchronizes visible grave heights immediately, including paused saves. The award is Sun-shroom, leading to 2-2. Night music uses the same `MusicSystem`, reproducing scheme 2's next-order drum entry and melody crossfade while preserving the separate drum phase in presentation saves.

Adventure 2-2 reuses the 2-1 night flow: 20 waves (flags at 10 and 20) of normal, conehead, buckethead, newspaper and flag zombies, the 2-1 grave layout, and graves that raise normal or conehead zombies 4000:4000 after the final wave. On the first adventure Dave says lines 1401–1402, with `{MONEY}` and `{UPGRADE_COST}` replaced by the current coins and the next seed-slot upgrade price. The award is Fume-shroom, leading to 2-3. Adventure 2-5 is Whack-a-Zombie (`LawnApp::IsWhackAZombieLevel`). The level rule `rules: {kind: "pvz:whack-a-zombie", count: 8, zombies, sun}` names the three zombies and the dropped sun, with 8 empty waves (the first wave's countdown is the level's usual `firstWaveTicks`). The mallet is the generic `boardClick` command, which goes to the rule (`Challenge::MouseDown`, `MouseDownWhackAZombie`): the front zombie within 45 of 20 above the click; a helm takes 900 to the helm only (`helmOnly` in `damageZombie`), otherwise the zombie dies at once with its loot, which may be three suns instead (`Board::DropLootPiece`, rarer with more sun), and the cursor is cleared. An emptied cursor is the mallet again only until the award drops, after which the mallet is put away (`Challenge::ClearCursor`, the rule's `cursorCleared`; the snapshot's `extensions["pvz:whack-a-zombie"].hammer`): it disappears with the blow that kills the last zombie and cannot swing again. The level starts with no sun, a fixed potato mine, grave buster and cherry bomb bank that starts ready (`openingRecharge: false`; planting still recharges, `SeedPacket::WasPlanted`) and no shovel (`tools: []`); a seed placed where it cannot go (a plant, a grave, or no grave for the grave buster) is dropped with the drop sound instead of staying in hand (`Board::MouseDownWithPlant`, the `seedDropped` event). Items take clicks 15 further around and 30 more below (`Coin::MouseHitTest`): the level's gameplay `itemClickPadding`, which the server's hit test (`itemAt`, shared) reads as the client does; elsewhere only the sun takes clicks 15 around (its item's `clickPadding`). ## Seeds and the seed chooser

The chooser grid is shared across levels. Every seed has a `position`, its place in its group (`shared/content/seedGroups.ts`, 8 columns in reading order from 0, 48 places a page), unique in the group. The groups come in this order: the original seeds, each Mod's, then the hidden cards. The base's seeds are the original group, the chooser grid (`SeedChooserScreen::GetSeedPositionInChooser`): their places are the original's plant seed types, plants only, 0-47, five rows (0-39) until a seed at 40-47 (the upgrade plants) is offered, then six rows 70 apart from y=123 (`SeedChooserScreen::Has7Rows`); 48 is the Imitater's. A place nobody takes stays an empty silhouette. Base seeds marked `"hidden": true` are the hidden cards: explode-o-nut, the two bowling nuts, every zombie seed, the grave seed and the crater seed. A Mod's seeds are its own group, numbered from 0, never in the grid nor hidden; Mods move anyone's seed by overriding its whole definition (`overrides.seeds`). A seed's `kind` says what it puts down: `plant` (with its `placement`), `zombie` (the original's `SEED_ZOMBIE_*`), `grave` or `crater`. A plant or zombie seed puts down the plant or zombie of its own ID unless its `id` names another, and a new kind of seed is one more definition with its own `kind`, leaving the others as they are. Every zombie has a zombie seed of its own ID (`pvz:normal` for the plain one): the plain, cone, pole vaulter, bucket, screen door and dancer ones cost what the original's do (50, 75, 75, 125, 100, 350), the flag, newspaper and backup dancer zombies, which the original has none for, 50, 75 and 100; they have no recharge and work on any level (through `/seed` or a level's `seedPackets`): they go into any cell of an active row, whatever stands there, 30 left of the cell (`Challenge::IZombiePlaceZombie`), and their packet draws the zombie at its presentation `packet` offset, scale and pose `clip` (the pole vaulter on `walk`, as the original's cache does). The grave seed `pvz:grave` (`"kind": "grave"`, 0 sun, no recharge; the original has none) puts a grave in any cell of an active row the way Grave Danger's come (`Challenge::GraveDangerSpawnGraveAt`): the plants there die and it rises from that tick; a cell that already has a grave refuses it (`/grave` still replaces one). It is the zombies' own; a packet of the plants' side gives the grave `team: "plants"`, and the zombies rising from it after the last wave are on that side too. The crater seed `pvz:crater` (`"kind": "crater"`, its crater's `craterTicks` 18000; the original has none) puts a crater in any cell of an active row but one with a crater or a grave, the plants there dying; it is the plants' own (`seedTeam`), a packet of the zombies' side turning the crater's picture round. A grave seed keeps off a crater, as `/grave` and a level's graves do (`Board::CanAddGraveStoneAt`). A pole vaulter facing right vaults right, the jump mirrored across both widths; one put down on a plant's cell does not vault back. The grid holds plant seeds only. A level's `conveyor` gives a belt and `seedPackets` a fixed bank; otherwise the bank follows the original (`Board::InitLevel`): it takes every available seed when they fit, and the chooser opens when there are more. Slots are `settings.seedBank.baseSlots` (6) plus the seed-slot upgrades bought, capped at `maxSlots` (10) and at the available seeds (`Board::GetNumSeedsInBank`); levels list no slot count or candidate pool. The client builds `maxSlots` packets up front, so a slot bought in the intro only shows one more. `server/seeds/seedChooser.ts` supplies the grid, validates choices and confirmation, and saves their order and initial cooldowns. A bank lists its slots in order and an empty one is `null`: while choosing, the chosen packets come one after another (taking one out moves the rest up); `finishSeedChoice` starts the level and leaves the unchosen slots empty, which only a Mod's Let's Rock taking fewer cards does, and a short bank from an older save gets its empty slots back on loading. An empty slot draws nothing and cannot be picked; `/seed` fills or empties any slot. `/repick` (`startRepick`) chooses the bank again in play, the level standing still meanwhile. A repick and the pause are two states of their own (a pause stops the chooser too and the player ends it; a repick stops only the level and its Let's Rock ends it; both may hold at once), but to the board they are one: what the client shows and allows on the lawn goes by `levelStandsStill(snapshot)`, never by `paused` or `repick` alone. A repick keeps its cards chosen (closed up over empty slots), with Survival's repick recharges (`CutScene::IsSurvivalRepick`): the bank keeps `repick` (its size, whether the level had opened the chooser, and the packets it held) while `choosing`, the server runs no gameplay ticks and refuses everything but the choice (`choosing-seeds`), a card chosen again takes its old packet's recharge back and any other is ready (`SeedPacket::SetPacketType`), and no chooser warning is given. After a repick's Let's Rock, `finishSeedChoice` marks it `confirmed` and the level keeps standing still until the client, its chooser lifted out and the bank back, sends `finishRepick`, which puts the bank back to the level's own, chosen or dealt. A repick is never saved (`SeedRepick` keeps the bank from before it): a save made during one holds that bank, or once confirmed the bank chosen, with the chooser state the level had, and loads into play. The client part holds none either: its frame is the server's world as the save holds it (`IntegratedGameServer.savedSnapshot`, from the same `savedSeedBank` as the save), so loading shows no chooser. The client drops the chooser in and lifts it out on its own ticks (`LevelSeedChooser.repickTick`) without moving the camera, moves the bank to its choosing place at x=0 as the chooser drops and back to 10 as it lifts (`SEED_BANK_OFFSET_X`, `SEED_BANK_OFFSET_X_END`; 25 ticks each way, on the original's slide times, `SEED_CHOOSER_SLIDE` in `LevelSeedChooser`), the chooser's cards resting in the bank riding with it (`setBankShift`), lifts the bank into the HUD right under the chooser meanwhile, the level award's layer under the bank (over the lawn's entities, the coins and the award, which would otherwise come between the bank and the chooser's cards in it; over the chooser it would hide them), then puts it back under the entities; Space leaves a repick alone as it does the intro's chooser, while the menu opens (`LevelController.isRepicking`), hides the Almanac and Shop buttons and the shovel, pauses the music (`LevelAudioPresenter.holdMusic`, which the pause menu's resume leaves paused), stands the lawn's animations still, and builds a chooser the first time a level that opened none needs one; Mods wrap `LevelView.attachSeedChooser` to add to every chooser, and take the mouse wheel through `LevelSeedChooser.onWheel` (the level root listens for it): a node of their own listening for any mouse event would take every press over it (`NodeEventProcessor._handleMouseDown`), such as the Menu button's. Chooser cards and their pictures draw in reading order, each over the one before, as `SeedChooserScreen::Draw` and `SeedBank::Draw` go left to right. `LevelSeedChooser` places each seed at its position, leaving locked ones' places empty, rather than by preview image indices and handles presentation using the shared clock. Use `/level pvz:seed-chooser-test` to test.

## Plants and zombies

Zombies have the original's two armor slots: the helm (`mHelmType`: cone 370, bucket 1100, football helmet 1400) and the shield (`mShieldType`: newspaper 150, screen door 1100), each written `{type, health}`; a zombie may wear both. Damage follows `Zombie::TakeDamage`: the shield first, then the helm, then the body. A shield takes no chill and counts 0.2 towards a wave's health, and the Fume-shroom's fumes pass it to hit the body as well; a helm and a shield that run out send `zombieHelmDropped` and `zombieShieldDropped`, and a mowed zombie drops whatever it wears first (`Zombie::MowDown`). The gameplay `animation` gives each clip's frames, the walk's ground track and the frames that mark bites and the fall, with `from` taking another zombie's clips; `vault` describes the pole vaulter's run, jump and landing. A projectile's `chillTicks` halves a zombie's animation, walk and bites, as the original's `ApplyAnimRate` does. The Cherry Bomb's and the Explode-o-nut's blasts char zombies into `pvz:zombie_charred`; a headless zombie turns black and stands for 300 ticks instead. New combat animations are registered in the `exports` of `tools/pvz/anim-defs.json`; `npm run build:content -- --pak /path/to/main.pak` converts them into `mods/pvz-base/assets/` and repacks the built-in ZIP, leaving the authored gameplay and presentation tables as they are.

`pvz:bowling-wallnut` and `pvz:bowling-explodenut` are plants and seeds of their own, configured in the gameplay and presentation JSON. Their kind is `bowlingnut` (`BowlingNut` on the server, `BowlingNutView` on the client), whose fields `direction` and `hits` are the way it rolls and the strikes so far. Their `bowling` must give the attack rect `attack` (`Plant::GetPlantAttackRect`); `bounceSpeed` (2), `damage` (1800), `helmDamage` (900) and `shieldDamage` (400) take `Plant::UpdateBowling`'s values unless a plant gives its own, and `bouncedBreaks` (the shields a whole strike breaks through after a bounce; the plain nut's `["door"]`) is empty unless given. The explosive nut goes off at the first touch and gives no damage. The settings are checked by exact keys as the content loads, not when a nut is planted. A nut rolls by the `ground` of its body layer's rolling clip (`wallnut_roll`'s `_ground`), with the same `groundStep` zombies walk by (`Reanimation::GetTrackVelocity`). Rolling down a row it is drawn with the row above (`BowlingNutView.drawnRow`, `Plant::UpdateBowling`), and its rolling sound when put down (`SOUND_BOWLING` in `Board::MouseDownWithPlant`) is the nut's own `plantedSounds`. Only the Wall-nut's art is reused; the plain Wall-nut takes no part in bowling. `occupiesCell: false` makes a moving plant hold no cell and be nothing to bite; `low: true` makes a low plant that hostile projectiles fly over (see [Teams](TEAMS.md)); the presentation's `layer: "projectile"` draws a rolling nut on its row's projectile layer (`Plant::CalcRenderOrder` on bowling levels).

## Coins

Coins follow `Board::DropLootPiece`: each zombie rolls 0–29999 once, when it loses its head or is mowed, burned or instantly killed, with chances scaled by its value (`zombieValue`: its `zombiePool` points, else the zombie's own `wave.points`, so a zombie the level does not pick drops by its value too; every zombie has a `wave`): silver (1 coin, shown as $10), gold (5) and diamond (100, gold until the first seed-slot upgrade). Nothing drops once the award has. Replays always drop coins; the first adventure from level 11 (2-1) on, without any per-level setting. 2-1's script adds `loot.firstCoinAfterWave`, which guarantees a first coin with a pickup arrow after wave 5, and `loot.savingsCap`, which stops drops that would bring the coins (including the remaining mowers' gold) to the upgrade price. Coins are `kind: "money"` items that spin once landed and fly to the bottom-left coin bank, which shows for 1000 ticks and fades, and also when the game is paused (not by the debug CLI). From 200 ticks after the award, each unused mower turns into a gold coin every 40 ticks from the bottom, with a rising note. The profile owns the purse: start and restore take the coins and seed-slot upgrades from it, and every change in the level (including `/money`) is written back at once. The `items` presentation table supplies item visuals, animations and sounds.

## Almanac

Implemented entities share their Almanac animation, body coordinates, scale and shadow with the level presentation definitions. Seed chooser previews also use idle clip coordinates directly. The almanac's plant page is the chooser's grid (`AlmanacDialog::GetSeedPosition`): a plant's place is its seed's `position`, its name its presentation's `visual.nameKey`. Its zombie windows are the gameplay `almanac` table, by zombie ID: `{place, startingLevel, spawnedOnly?, afterAdventure?}`; a zombie's name is its presentation's `nameKey`. The presentation `almanac` table gives the ground each plant or zombie stands on (`ground`: `day`, `night`, `pool`, `nightpool`, `roof` or `ice`, the day's without an entry) and an upgrade plant's packet (`upgrade`).

The almanac's zombie windows (each zombie's place and debut level and so on) are the gameplay `almanac` table and its plants the chooser's grid; a plant's cost and recharge are its seed definition's `sunCost` and `cooldownTicks`, as the original's almanac reads `gPlantDefs`. Like the store's, the tables are read with the rest of the gameplay definitions (Mods included), and the app builds the backend's catalogs from them; `server/menu/almanacCatalog` only applies the unlock rules. The almanac follows the current profile: it lists only the plants whose seeds the player owns (the profile's `seeds` plus `seedBank.startingSeeds`, as for the chooser) and are installed, and leaves the others undrawn. A zombie shows from its `gZombieDefs` `mStartingLevel` (`ZombieIsShown`; backup dancers, the bobsled team and the imp only after that level), otherwise its window is blank and not clickable, and is described only after that level (`ZombieHasDescription`); before then the description box centers `NOT_ENCOUNTERED_YET`. A finished adventure shows every zombie. Not yet implemented: the original also describes a zombie defeated since the player was selected (`gZombieDefeated`), and draws the Yeti as a silhouette before it can spawn; here the Yeti stays hidden until then.
